🖐 Left-hand & right-hand finger guide

Use this color-coded reference to master standard US QWERTY touch-typing finger zones. Always return your fingers to the home row (A S D F and J K L ;) after each keystroke.

🫲 Left hand

Pinky (LP)` · 1 · Q · A · Z · Left Shift
Ring (LR)2 · W · S · X
Middle (LM)3 · E · D · C
Index (LI)4 · 5 · R · F · V · T · G · B
Thumb (TH)Space bar

🫱 Right hand

Index (RI)6 · 7 · Y · H · N · U · J · M
Middle (RM)8 · I · K · ,
Ring (RR)9 · O · L · .
Pinky (RP)0 · - · = · P · [ ] \ · ; · ' · / · Enter · Shift
Thumb (TH)Space bar
Pro Touch-Typing Tip: When typing an uppercase letter or top-row symbol (such as !, ?, or "), hold Shift with the pinky of the opposite hand while pressing the target key.

⌨ Home-row anchor positioning

ASDF JKL;

Rest your left fingers gently on A, S, D, F and your right fingers on J, K, L, ;. Feel for the small raised tactile bumps on F and J to align your hands without looking down.
